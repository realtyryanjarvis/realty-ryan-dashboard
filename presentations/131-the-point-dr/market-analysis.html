The Strategic Read

The $2.1M price band captured 13 of 21 total showings — that's the active buyer pool in The Point right now. The $3.2M range (131 The Point's prior list price) received just 1 showing in 30 days. The $2.9M–$3.1M range had zero showings — not because buyers don't exist there, but because there are no listings. At $2,600,000, 131 The Point enters the market at $2.6M — a decisive step below the $3M psychological threshold — priced $110K above the only other non-waterfront active listing (1812 Brawley School Rd at $2,490,000), with a clear quality and size premium to justify every dollar of that gap.
