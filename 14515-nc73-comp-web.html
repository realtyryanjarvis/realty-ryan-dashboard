The Property
2.13 Acres of Unobstructed
Lake Norman

This is a rare, flat, fully cleared parcel with direct lake access on Lake Norman — wide open water views with no obstructions. The lot is pier-eligible, though no pier is currently installed. It fronts NC Hwy 73 with an approximate 100-foot public right-of-way and sits entirely within Mecklenburg County zoning.

At 2.13 acres, this is an uncommonly large waterfront lot — one that offers genuine flexibility for a luxury custom estate, with the water frontage and views to justify a generational build.
