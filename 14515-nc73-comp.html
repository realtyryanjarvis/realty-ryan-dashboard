Pricing Philosophy

Working Backwards From Value

Work Backwards From Value
The right way to price land is top-down. Start with what a finished luxury lakefront estate would sell for, subtract the cost to build it, and what remains is what the land is worth.
Finished Home Value: $11M–$15M+
A custom luxury estate on 2.13 acres with wide open water views would command $11M–$15M+ on Lake Norman. A Peninsula property sold at $11.5M in 2024.
This Will Take a Special Buyer
The right buyer is building a $7.5M+ estate — $3.75M for land, $3.75M+ to build. They chose privacy and views over a prestigious zip code. That buyer exists.

Working backwards: $11M–$12M finished home value, minus $4M–$5M to build, minus $400K–$600K in site work and pier, minus $300K–$500K for location and road — leaves $3.5M–$4.5M in residual land value. $3,750,000 sits squarely in the middle.

⚠ NC-73 Corridor Note: Planned Highway Expansion

NC Highway 73 is slated for expansion in this corridor. For a buyer seeking a private lakefront estate, this warrants disclosure — increased traffic volume and construction activity will be a near-term reality along the subject's road frontage. However, highway expansions also signal long-term corridor growth: improved access, rising land values, and increased development pressure in the area. The subject lot's depth and setback provide meaningful buffer. A sophisticated buyer should factor in both the near-term construction impact and the long-term appreciation this infrastructure investment typically drives. The expansion also helps explain why developer interest in this corridor is accelerating — as noted below.
